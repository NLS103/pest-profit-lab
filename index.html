FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be a PCO Bookkeepers client?

No. Profit Lab works on its own. Bookkeeping clients get a head start because their data is already clean and current.

How long does it take?

Once we have your data, the first analysis usually takes 2–3 weeks. You get a full pricing report and a walkthrough call with your analyst.

What if my data is messy?

Cleaning data is one of the things we do best, and one of the things clients like most.

Does it matter what software I use?

We work mainly with FieldRoutes, PestPac and QuickBooks Online. If you use something else, we'll talk it through on the first call.

Will this work in my market?

The analysis uses your own costs: your overhead, labor and materials, not national averages. You decide what to do with the recommendations.
